FAQ

Questions, answered plainly.

Is Clipps trading real money?

No. The current Clipps demo is a simulation that runs in your browser. Market prices, agent decisions and fills are generated locally, and no orders are sent anywhere.

Are BTC, ETH, NVDA and the other markets live?

No. They are example markets with familiar names so the workspace is easy to read. Their prices are simulated and do not track the real instruments.

What do the agents actually do?

Each demo agent has a stated strategy, a status and a simulated signal-confidence value. In this simulation they place randomised paper trades labelled with their strategy’s rationale. It shows the workflow, not trading skill or performance.

Can I connect my own agent?

Not yet. If you are building agents and want to talk about what Clipps should support, email support@clipps.si.

Why “superintelligent agents”?

It is our long-term vision: a terminal built for very capable autonomous agents, where people can still see and steer what those agents do. Today’s demo shows the interface ideas, not those agents.

Is any of this investment advice?

No. Nothing on this site is investment advice.
